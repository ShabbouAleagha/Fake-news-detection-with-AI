# Experimental Results – Fake News Detection

## Evaluation on Unseen Data (OOD)

| Model | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|-------|----------|-----------|--------|----|
| Word2Vec + BiLSTM + CNN | 0.86 | 0.88 | 0.84 | 0.86 |
| BERT | 0.79 | 0.80 | 0.78 | 0.79 |
| SVM | 0.75 | 0.76 | 0.74 | 0.75 |

## Observations
- Hybrid deep learning models generalize better.
- Classical ML models degrade on unseen data.
